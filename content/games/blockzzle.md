## No-Install Browser Play

Blockzzle is built for quick web play. There is no install step, no login requirement, and no email address needed before you start playing.

## How The Block Puzzle Works

The core loop is about placing block pieces carefully, making room for the next shapes, and clearing full lines. Each line clear opens space on the board, and stronger placement choices help you keep a run alive while you chase a better score.

## A Simple Starting Strategy

1. Before placing a block, look for a row or column you can complete.
2. Compare placements that leave a connected open area with ones that create small isolated gaps.
3. After a line clears, look at the new space before choosing your next placement.

For your next run, focus on keeping room for later pieces rather than filling the first available gap. A clear opens space, but the shape of the space left behind matters too.
