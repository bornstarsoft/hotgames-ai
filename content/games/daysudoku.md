DaySudoku is a free browser-based daily Sudoku game. Play one puzzle a day with no sign-up or download required.

[Play DaySudoku](https://daysudoku.com/)

## How The Daily Sudoku Works {#what-key9-means}

Fill empty cells with numbers from 1 to 9. Each row, column, and 3x3 box must contain each number exactly once. The starting numbers stay fixed.

## A Simple Starting Strategy

1. Look for a row, column, or box with only a few empty cells.
2. Pick one empty cell and rule out numbers already in its row, column, and box.
3. Enter a number when only one possibility remains. If several fit, check another cell rather than guessing.

For your next puzzle, focus on finding one certain move before looking further across the board.

[Read the official Sudoku rules](https://daysudoku.com/how-to-play/).
