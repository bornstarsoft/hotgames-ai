Before you play

Do I need an account or a download?

No. Blockzzle, Ringzzle, and DaySudoku are free to play in the browser without signing up or downloading an app. Each Play link opens the game's own site.

Which one is a daily puzzle?

DaySudoku offers one free daily Sudoku puzzle. Fill each row, column, and 3x3 box with the numbers 1-9 exactly once. Blockzzle and Ringzzle are separate score-chasing puzzles.

Are these pictures actual game screens?

No. The covers are AI-generated editorial illustrations of each puzzle type. Open a game to see its actual board and controls.

Where can I find another kind of game?

Explore the browser game selection, browse more puzzle games, or return to your saved games. Saved games belong to this browser, not an online account.
